How We Work

From Mine to Masterpiece

Our Promise

A Journey of Absolute Trust

Every gem that bears the Encrozaa name has passed through four rigorous stages — from the riverbed deposits of Ratnapura to your hands. We believe that true luxury demands total transparency, and we honour that at every step.

Step by Step

The Four Stages

01
Step 01
Source & Discover

Our gemologists travel deep into the gem fields of Ratnapura and Elahera, working directly with licensed mine owners to gain first access to exceptional rough.

Direct mine partnerships in Ratnapura & Elahera
Strict ethical & environmental standards
First-look access to top-quality rough
02
Step 02
Evaluate & Grade

Each stone undergoes expert assessment by our in-house gemologists. We prioritise unheated, untreated specimens exhibiting vivid saturation and exceptional colour distribution.

Colour saturation & distribution analysis
Treatment screening under UV & magnification
Investment-grade rarity assessment
03
Step 03
Certify & Document

Every selected gem is sent to a world-leading gemological laboratory — GRS, GIA, or AGL — for independent certification confirming origin, treatment status, and quality.

Certified by GRS, GIA & AGL laboratories
Full origin & treatment documentation
Provenance chain recorded end-to-end
04
Step 04
Deliver Securely

Your gem travels from our Colombo vaults to your door in bespoke packaging, fully insured against loss or damage, with real-time tracking throughout.

Fully insured international shipping
Bespoke presentation packaging
Real-time tracking & delivery confirmation
