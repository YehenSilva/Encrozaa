How We Work

From Mine to Masterpiece

01
Source & Discover
Our gemologists travel to Ratnapura and Elahera, working directly with mine owners to identify stones of extraordinary quality.
02
Evaluate & Grade
Every stone is independently certified by GRS, GIA, or AGL — assessing cut, clarity, colour, and treatment status.
03
Document & Certify
Full provenance documentation and lab reports accompany every gem, ensuring complete transparency for the buyer.
04
Deliver Securely
Insured, discreet worldwide shipping in bespoke packaging — your gem arrives as precious as it left our vaults.
