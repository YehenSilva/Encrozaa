Our Heritage

The Spirit of
Ceylon

For centuries, Sri Lanka has been known as 'Ratna Deepa'—the Island of Gems. At Encrozaa, we honor this ancient legacy by sourcing only the most exceptional specimens from the island's legendary riverbeds.

Our philosophy is rooted in authenticity. We specialize in unheated, natural stones that maintain their original character, certified by world-leading gemological laboratories.

Our Principles

The Encrozaa Standard

Ethical Sourcing

Every gem is sourced directly from artisanal mines in Ratnapura, ensuring fair labor and environmental respect.

Timeless Rarity

We curate stones based on their "investment grade" potential—prioritizing unheated stones and vivid saturations.

Absolute Trust

Transparency is our bedrock. Full laboratory reports accompany every stone, detailing origin and treatment.

Guaranteed Authenticity

Certified Excellence

To provide our international clientele with absolute peace of mind, every gem in our collection is independently graded by the world’s most prestigious laboratories including GRS, GIA, and AGL.
